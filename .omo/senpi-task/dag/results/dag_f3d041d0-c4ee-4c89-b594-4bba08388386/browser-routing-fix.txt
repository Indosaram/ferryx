### 2. Implementation

Updated `handleDragOver` in `ui/src/components/TerminalSplitView.tsx` to skip triggering `onActivateTab` when `previewTabId === normalizedLayout.activeTabId`:

```tsx
    if (
      !previewTabId ||
      previewTabId === active.tabId ||
      previewTabId === normalizedLayout.activeTabId ||
      previewedTabIdRef.current === previewTabId
    )
      return;
    previewedTabIdRef.current = previewTabId;
    onActivateTab(previewTabId);
```
