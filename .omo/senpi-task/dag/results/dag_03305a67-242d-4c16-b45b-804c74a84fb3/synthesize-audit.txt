  * `ui/src/state/workspaceStore.ts:109` & `ui/src/App.tsx:110-120`: `useWorkspaceStore` initializes in-memory state via `useReducer` on mount. Switching projects via `handleSelectProject` (`ui/src/App.tsx:384-394`) changes `activeProject.workspaceId`, but when `loadSession` encounters the overwritten session file, `deserializeWorkspaceState` returns `null` (`ui/src/lib/sessionPersistence.ts:121-122`), preventing `restoreWorkspace` (`ui/src/App.tsx:137`) and leaving the prior project's worktrees, tabs, and sessions active in state.
  * `ui/src/App.tsx:140-160`: On restart, dead sessions are respawned against `foundWorktree ? worktreeIdentity(foundWorktree) : null` (`ui/src/App.tsx:146-150`). If workspace isolation broke prior to restart, respawned backend PTYs bind to leaked worktree paths and cwd contexts.
