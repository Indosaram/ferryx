### Outcome Summary

The P1 keyboard-shortcut parity gaps have been closed and verified end-to-end:
1. **`Cmd+]` / `Cmd+[`** (`terminal.focusNext` / `terminal.focusPrevious`): Cycles pane focus forward/backward across split panes in the active tab.
2. **`Cmd+F`** (`terminal.search`): Integrates `@xterm/addon-search` into `terminalHostManager` and mounts a search overlay on the focused terminal pane with incremental search, next/prev navigation (Enter/Shift+Enter or arrow buttons), match indicator, and Escape / close dismissal with terminal refocusing.
3. **`Cmd+,`** (`settings.toggle`): Toggles the Settings dialog from anywhere in the shell.
