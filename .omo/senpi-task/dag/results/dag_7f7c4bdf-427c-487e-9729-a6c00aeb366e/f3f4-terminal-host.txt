### Summary of Changes

1. **Remove Duplicate Direct Registrations (F3)** (`ui/src/lib/terminalHostManager.ts`):
   - Removed direct closure fallback registrations for `onBell` and `onTitleChange`.
   - `terminal.onTitleChange` and `terminal.onBell` now delegate solely to `this.instances.get(session.id)?.onTitleChange?.(title)` and `this.instances.get(session.id)?.onBell?.()`, ensuring only the latest handler fires and stale closures never execute.
2. **Terminal Host Destroy on Tab and Pane Close (F4)** (`ui/src/state/workspaceStore.ts`):
   - Invokes `terminalHostManager.destroy(sessionId)` on tab close (both single-tab replacement and multi-tab paths) as well as unshared pane close paths.
3. **Unsubscribe Previous Output Subscription on Backend ID Change (F5)** (`ui/src/lib/terminalHostManager.ts`):
   - Maintained `unsubscribeOutput` disposable on `TerminalInstance`.
   - In `updateSession`, when `backendSessionId` changes, `inst.unsubscribeOutput?.()` is invoked prior to establishing a new output subscription.
   - In `destroy`, `inst.unsubscribeOutput?.()` is cleaned up.
4. **PaneResizeDivider Cleanup on Unmount** (`ui/src/components/TerminalSplitView.tsx`):
   - Added `useEffect` cleanup hook to `PaneResizeDivider` holding a `cleanupRef` that removes `pointermove`, `pointerup`, and `pointercancel` window listeners and clears `document.body.style.cursor` on unmount mid-drag.
5. **Preview Branding Banner** (`ui/src/lib/terminalHostManager.ts`):
   - Changed non-Tauri preview banner output from `rorca` to `Ferryx`.
