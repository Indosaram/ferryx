### 2. Contradictions Between Discovery Lanes

1. **State Reducer vs DnD Pipeline**:
   - *Hypothesis*: Pane splitting fails because tree mutations destroy PTY sessions or corrupt node ratios.
   - *Resolution*: State tests pass (67/67). The failure is strictly upstream in DnD registration (`PaneEdgeDropZones` unmounted), validation (`isWorkspaceDropData` rejecting `pane-edge`), and priority sorting (`dropPriority` returning `100`).
2. **Whole-Tab Group Split vs Inner-Pane Subtree Split**:
   - *Hypothesis*: Dragging a tab to a split target should behave identically for terminal and browser tabs.
   - *Resolution*: Terminal tabs support both levels (tab-group split and pane-leaf split). Browser tabs only support tab-group splits (`MOVE_TAB_TO_SPLIT`). When a terminal tab is dropped onto an inner pane edge, it must dispatch `MOVE_TAB_TO_PANE_SPLIT` to graft the subtree into that exact leaf rather than splitting the top-level tab group.
3. **20% Hit Target vs 50% Split Result**:
   - *Hypothesis*: The visual overlay in the symptom screenshot was misplaced due to broken coordinate transformation math.
   - *Resolution*: Coordinate math is exact. The indicator highlights a 20% edge collision zone (`h-[20%]`), whereas the resulting split ratio is 50/50 (`ratio: 0.5`).
4. **Browser Lifecycle Leaks**:
   - *Hypothesis*: Dragging browser tabs across splits causes native child webview memory or visibility leaks.
   - *Resolution*: Serialized promise chaining in `browserTauri.ts` prevents race conditions during group moves. Browser tabs are excluded from inner pane subtrees by design.
