### 3. DnD Target Semantics & Visual Feedback

#### A. Drop Target Geometry & Zones

- **Orca Original (`rename-file-JaIUF221.js:4149-4175` & `Terminal-qm6WvB4Q.js:2356-2384`)**:
  - `resolvePaneColumnEdgeZone` checks pointer distance against a `20%` outer margin:
    - `localX < panelRect.width * 0.2` => `"left"`
    - `localX > panelRect.width * 0.8` => `"right"`
    - Inside tab body: `localY < bodyRect.height * 0.2` => `"up"`, `localY > bodyRect.height * 0.8` => `"down"`.
  - Inner area resolves to `"center"` (drop into tab bar / group body).
- **`orca-lite` (`ui/src/components/tab-dnd/SplitEdgeDropZone.tsx:63-74` & `TabGroupDropSurface.tsx:48-64`)**:
  - Exact match for edge thresholds:
    - `"left"`: `inset-y-0 left-0 w-[20%]`
    - `"right"`: `inset-y-0 right-0 w-[20%]`
    - `"top"`: `left-[20%] right-[20%] top-0 h-[20%]`
    - `"bottom"`: `bottom-0 left-[20%] right-[20%] h-[20%]`

### 4. Known-Good Logic Adaptable Without Extraneous Dependencies

1. **Pre-Drag Activation Snapshot & Restore**:
   - Orca Original (`rename-file-JaIUF221.js:4330-4355`) captures `captureTabDragActivationSnapshot` on `onDragStart` and selectively restores focus via `restoreSourceGroupActiveTabAfterCrossGroupDrop` only if drop fails or targets another group.
   - `orca-lite` currently implements `dragSnapshotRef` (`ui/src/components/TerminalSplitView.tsx:173-214`), which safely restores `activeTabId` when a drag is canceled.
2. **Non-Destructive Subtree Grafting**:
   - `moveTabIntoPaneSplit` (`ui/src/state/tabPaneDrop.ts:20-109`) and `splitLeafWithSubtree` (`ui/src/state/paneTree.ts:46-60`) allow terminal sessions to migrate across tabs and splits without triggering process exits or xterm remounts.
3. **Collision Detection Priority**:
   - `workspaceCollisionDetection` with `dropPriority` (`ui/src/components/tab-dnd/tabDragTypes.ts:172-184`) ensures pane-edge zones take precedence over general tab group surfaces during pointer collisions.

### 5. Summary of Differences & Equivalence Status

- **`.orca-worktrees/rorca-qa/e2e-qa/ui/src/` vs `orca-lite`**: The QA worktree's `ui/src/` contains an obsolete single-level primary/secondary layout reducer (`ENABLE_SPLIT`, `DISABLE_SPLIT`). `orca-lite` is significantly more advanced, having full tab-group and nested pane-tree capabilities.
- **Original Orca (`original-dist`) vs `orca-lite`**: `orca-lite` achieves semantic and visual parity with Orca's tab-drag and split behavior, matching the 20% edge threshold geometry, the `"New split"` overlay badge, and session-preserving pane moves.
