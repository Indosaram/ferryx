### Summary of Findings:
1. **`F-daemon-session-01` (High)**: `DaemonClient::send_request` reconnects and executes a full handshake roundtrip per request, introducing high latency and socket churn on terminal input paths (`write_terminal`, `resize_terminal`, `signal_terminal`).
2. **`F-daemon-session-02` (High)**: `DaemonServer::pump_stream` serializes raw `Vec<u8>` PTY broadcast chunks as JSON integer arrays with unbuffered socket writes, causing ~3-4x data expansion and allocation pressure during high output bursts.
3. **`F-daemon-session-03` (Medium)**: `DaemonServer::handle_client` executes synchronous session persistence and double `fsync` calls directly on Tokio async worker threads without `tokio::task::spawn_blocking`.
4. **`F-daemon-session-04` (Medium)**: `save_session_to_path` performs full-tree pretty-printed serialization (`serde_json::to_string_pretty`) and redundant parent directory `fsync` on every persistence cycle.
