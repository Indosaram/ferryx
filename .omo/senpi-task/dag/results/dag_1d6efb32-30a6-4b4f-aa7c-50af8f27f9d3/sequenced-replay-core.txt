### Implementation Summary
1. **Monotonic `u64` Output Sequences**: `OutputChunk` structure carrying `sequence: u64` and `bytes: Vec<u8>`. `BoundedBuffer` assigns contiguous 1-indexed monotonic sequences per session.
2. **Bounded Sequence Range**: `BoundedBuffer` maintains chunk sequence bounds via `start_sequence()` and `end_sequence()`, evicting oldest chunks on capacity overflow while preserving exact sequence range.
3. **Subscriber-First Attachment Snapshots**: `subscribe_with_sequence()` registers the broadcast receiver *before* taking the bounded buffer snapshot within the critical section to prevent lost or duplicated chunks during attachment.
4. **Replay After Sequence**: `snapshot_after(after_sequence)` filters and returns only chunks with `sequence > after_sequence`.
5. **Explicit Replay Gap Detection**: When `requested_after_sequence + 1 < available_from_sequence`, `ReplayGap { requested_after_sequence, available_from_sequence }` is returned alongside available history.
6. **Backward Compatibility**: Preserved raw `Vec<u8>` publish, subscribe, and attach APIs for existing callers while dual-broadcasting to sequence and raw receivers.
