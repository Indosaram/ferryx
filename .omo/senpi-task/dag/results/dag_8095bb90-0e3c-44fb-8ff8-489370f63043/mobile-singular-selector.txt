### Privacy guarantees

- Exactly one declared terminal is rendered.
- Background or malformed extra sessions are never listed, titled, attached, or exposed.
- Explicit `activeTerminal: null` remains authoritative even if a legacy sessions array contains a live background PTY.
- Ambiguous multi-session legacy responses produce no mirror rather than guessing.
- Terminal titles are not rendered, preventing hidden-session title leakage.
- `repoRoot`, worktree paths, CWDs, and absolute local paths are ignored.
- Context text is rejected when it resembles an absolute filesystem path.
- Selection requests transmit only safe workspace IDs and optional worktree slugs.
