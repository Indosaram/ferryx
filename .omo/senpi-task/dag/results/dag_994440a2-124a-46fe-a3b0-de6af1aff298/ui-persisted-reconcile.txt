### Summary of Changes

1. **Types (`ui/src/lib/types.ts`)**:
   - Added `daemonEpoch?: string | null` to `TerminalSessionSummary`.
   - Maintained decimal-string `daemonEpoch` and `lastOutputSequence` fields on `TerminalSession` and `PersistedTerminalSession`.

2. **Persistence & Reconciliation (`ui/src/lib/sessionPersistence.ts`)**:
   - `serializeWorkspaceState`: Serializes decimal-string `daemonEpoch` and `lastOutputSequence` separately from frontend `localSessionId` and native `backendSessionId`.
   - `deserializeWorkspaceState`: Reconciles live sessions against the current daemon `ListSessions` response:
     - When both `daemonEpoch` and `backendSessionId` match a live daemon session, `backendSessionId`, `daemonEpoch`, `lastOutputSequence`, and lifecycle (`working`) are preserved along with local IDs and pane tree mappings.
     - On daemon epoch change or missing backend session ID in the live daemon list, marks the session as exited/lost (`backendSessionId: null`, `lifecycle: "exited"`, `daemonEpoch: null`, `lastOutputSequence: null`) without automatic respawn while preserving pane hierarchy and tab ownership.

3. **Workspace Restore Coordinator (`ui/src/state/workspaceRestore.ts`)**:
   - Updated `defaultListLiveBackendSessionIds` to retrieve live summaries (including `daemonEpoch`) via native query and transport.
   - Preserves StrictMode idempotency ensuring at most one disk restore across double-mounts and immediate bypass for HMR-recovered workspaces.

4. **Workspace Store & HMR (`ui/src/state/workspaceStore.ts`, `ui/src/state/workspaceStore.hmrRetention.test.tsx`)**:
   - HMR retention retains `daemonEpoch`, `lastOutputSequence`, backend IDs, and layout state in-memory across module reload without invoking `spawnTerminal` or `closeTerminal`.
