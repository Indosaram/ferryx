### 1. Session Persistence & Updater Guard Architecture

#### Session Persistence Lifecycle:
1. **Data Model**: As defined in `SESSION_PERSISTENCE_AND_UPDATER_PLAN.md`, the persistent session structure (`PersistedWorkspaceSession`) captures:
   - `version: 1`
   - `activeWorkspaceId: string`
   - `workspaces`: Record of `workspaceId`, `repoRoot`, worktree metadata snapshots, active layout (`splitMode`, `primaryTabId`, `secondaryTabId`, `tabs`), and `terminalSessions`.
2. **Backend Storage**:
   - Location: `app_data_dir() / session_state.json`.
   - Atomic disk write semantics: Write to `.tmp` file first, followed by atomic rename and `.backup` fallback on corrupt state detection.
   - Rust IPC Commands: `cmd_session_save`, `cmd_session_load`, `cmd_session_clear`.
3. **Restoration Cycle**:
   - On app launch, `cmd_session_load()` is invoked.
   - Repositories are re-registered in `WorkspaceRegistry`.
   - Missing/moved worktrees degrade gracefully to `repoRoot`.
   - Layout and tabs are restored, and logical terminal sessions are respawned cleanly with fresh PTY instances in the restored CWD.

#### Updater & Beforeunload Guard:
1. **Safety Barrier**: The updater or window-close flow triggers `registerWindowCloseGuard`.
2. **Persistence Flush**: Before allowing process exit/restart, `workspaceStore` state is flushed immediately via `cmd_session_save()`.
3. **Safe Shutdown**: PTY sessions are cleanly signaled and terminated, and the restart proceeds only after the persistence checkpoint has durably committed.
