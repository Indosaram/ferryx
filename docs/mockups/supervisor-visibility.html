Three rules

  • Where it watches shows inside the pane, not in the sidebar tree. A supervised pane gets a 26px bar above the terminal. Unsupervised panes (zsh here) have none. The bar appears or goes away only when you supervise a worktree or stop, so the terminal resizes only at those moments.
  • When it acts is the loudest moment. The same bar switches to the active fill and shows the exact text it sent with Take over. For those seconds the worktree row's one state slot reads supervisor typing in foreground, and the tab adds the eye.
  • What it is doing overall lives in its own sidebar tab, Supervisor: goal and next step per worktree, the audit log (send / answer / wait / stop), and Pause.

States

  • Watching: the rows stay as they are. The only signal is the bar in the pane.
  • You type: it holds its message until 3s after your last key (HUMAN_TYPING_WINDOW_MS). The bar says so, and you can discard the held message.
  • Handed to you: it goes through the existing Inbox count and row, plus one line saying why it was left to you.
  • Paused: every bar says paused, and so does the Supervisor tab.
  • Nothing supervised: no bars at all. The Supervisor tab offers one action to start.

Contract

  • Only tokens from ui/DESIGN.md. No new hue, no state borders. Selection and acting are both a fill.
  • Sidebar rows keep one slot. Priority: needs input > supervisor typing > running > unread.
  • Status dots stay agent state only.
